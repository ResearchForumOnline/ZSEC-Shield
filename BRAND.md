# Zero Security brand system

## Product names

- **Zero Security** is the Windows antivirus programme and product family.
- **ZSEC Shield** remains the current scanner/core lineage.
- **Zero Browser** is the browser product.
- **ZeroQ Shields** is the reusable blocking and privacy engine.
- **Zero Qbrowser** is a working codename, not the public canonical name.

Canonical public routes are `https://talktoai.org/zero-security/` and
`https://talktoai.org/zero-browser/`. Naming still requires normal trademark and
store-listing clearance before a paid release.

## Visual language

- Near-black foundations: `#07100f` and `#0b1715`.
- Protection green: `#18e0a7`.
- Boundary cyan: `#55ffd0`.
- Evidence blue: `#238cff`.
- Quiet text: `#9cb7b0`.
- Directional arcs and a zero-shaped protected core communicate the ZBA
  entering, boundary, and emerging lifecycle without implying that the symbol
  itself supplies cryptographic strength.

The SVG mark is the deterministic source for UI icons and monochrome variants.
The generated raster artwork is for launch surfaces and should not be redrawn
by tracing third-party browser or antivirus branding.

## Public voice

Calm, precise, and evidence-led. Prefer “protected locally,” “authenticated
encrypted vault,” “best-effort ad protection,” and “full antivirus programme in
development.” Avoid “unhackable,” “military grade,” “quantum encryption,”
“blocks everything,” or unsupported comparison claims.

## Image-generation provenance

The two original raster assets in `assets/brand/` were generated for this
project with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 21 August 2026. They
contain no requested third-party logo, product mark, or readable text.

Hero prompt summary: launch artwork with an abstract protective boundary,
three directional phases, a luminous zero core, browser geometry, and a shield;
deep near-black, emerald, electric blue, and white; no text or third-party
branding.

Icon prompt summary: square app icon combining a zero-shaped core and protective
shield, optimized for small-size clarity in near-black, emerald, and blue; no
text or third-party branding.
