# ZSEC Antivirus 0.3.35: reliable automatic monitoring

Repairs three legitimate watcher events rejected by the desktop bridge: initial metadata inventory completion and superseded temporary files (single and batch). The observer now survives normal startup and disappearing downloads. Malformed and unknown events remain rejected.

Inventory access errors remain incomplete. Setup progress is visibly distinct from verified coverage. Automatic retries keep the current backoff; transient recovery stays in the window rather than producing repeated notifications. Persistent monitor failures still notify, with health alerts limited to one per category every thirty minutes. Rule detections retain their separate notification policy.

The remembered monitoring choice, Windows StartupTask integration, tray lifetime and standard-folder selection remain active. Microsoft Defender or another supported primary antivirus supplies pre-access enforcement. ZSEC performs post-change inspection while its process runs.

Publication, Store certification and installed acceptance are recorded separately in the delivery receipt.
